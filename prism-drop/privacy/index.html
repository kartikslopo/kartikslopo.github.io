Prism Drop Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026

Prism Drop is a mobile game made by an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. We don't ask for your name, email or any account.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your current level, best score, difficulty and sound setting on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising

Prism Drop is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent ad fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • approximate location derived from your IP address
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system and app version
  • how you interact with ads, and crash or performance data related to ads

This data is sent to Google, not to us. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Ad privacy on the game's start screen.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads. On iPhone, you can turn off ad tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the game.

Children

Prism Drop is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we'll help remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
